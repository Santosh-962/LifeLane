🛡️ Safety Failure Cases & Edge-Case Security Audits 4 of 4 Verified PASS
1. Backend Server Down PASS

Connection timeout is caught; signal controllers immediately revert to fixed 40s/3s/2s plan without skipping all-red clearance.

2. Camera Stream Missing PASS

If YOLO camera packets stop or delay (>3s), system flags FALLBACK_FIXED mode and auto-resumes once fresh video telemetry reconnects.

3. Fake Emergency Blocked PASS

Unauthorized vehicles without valid 112 IDs or unregistered fleet plates are rejected immediately with security audit logging. 0 signals altered.

4. Dual Conflicting Emergencies PASS

When two emergency vehicles reach the intersection simultaneously (South vs West), LifeLane arbitrates with mandatory 2s all-red clearance between green phases.
